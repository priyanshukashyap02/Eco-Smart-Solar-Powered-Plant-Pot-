# 🌱 Eco Smart Solar-Powered Plant Pot

An eco-friendly smart plant pot designed to automatically monitor plant conditions and provide water when required. The system uses solar energy and sensors to reduce water wastage and support sustainable plant care.

## 🚀 Features

- ☀️ Solar-powered operation
- 💧 Automatic irrigation based on soil moisture
- 🌡️ Temperature and humidity monitoring
- 🌱 Real-time plant condition monitoring
- ⚡ Energy-efficient and eco-friendly design
- 💦 Helps reduce unnecessary water consumption

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

Arduino UNO, C/C++, Soil Moisture Sensor, DHT11/DHT22, LDR Sensor, Mini Water Pump, Solar Panel, Relay Module, Embedded Systems, IoT

## 🔧 Components Used

- Arduino UNO
- Soil Moisture Sensor
- DHT11/DHT22 Temperature & Humidity Sensor
- LDR Sensor
- Mini DC Water Pump
- Relay Module
- Solar Panel
- Battery
- Jumper Wires
- Plant Pot
- Water Reservoir

## ⚙️ Working

1. The **soil moisture sensor** continuously checks the moisture level of the soil.
2. When the soil becomes too dry, the Arduino activates the **water pump** through the relay.
3. The pump supplies water to the plant automatically.
4. The **DHT11/DHT22 sensor** monitors temperature and humidity.
5. The **LDR sensor** detects surrounding light intensity.
6. The system operates using **solar energy**, making it energy-efficient and environmentally friendly.
